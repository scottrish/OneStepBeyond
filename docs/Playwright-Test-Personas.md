# Playwright Test Personas

## Persona 1 — Student

### Name

Alex Carter

### Profile

- Age: 14
- Grade: 9
- Diagnosed ADHD
- Significant executive functioning challenges
- Bright and capable but inconsistent
- Plays varsity freshman football with practice **five afternoons each week**, making after-school time scarce.
- Recently started a long-distance relationship and spends significant time texting, messaging and FaceTime with his girlfriend.

### Characteristics

- Frequently forgets assignments.
- Starts homework later than intended.
- Underestimates large projects.
- Easily overwhelmed when several assignments are due together.
- Often intends to "just reply to one message" and loses track of time.
- Highly motivated by social interaction.
- Values football and is committed to attending every practice.
- Prefers simple interfaces.
- Uses an iPhone constantly.
- Rarely uses a laptop except when required for schoolwork.

### Goals

- Know what to do next.
- Finish homework before relaxing.
- Stay academically eligible while fully participating in football.
- Have time to talk with his girlfriend without feeling guilty.
- Stop forgetting assignments.
- Feel less stressed.

### Frustrations

- Large overwhelming task lists.
- Losing an entire evening without realizing where the time went.
- Football practices leaving little time for homework.
- Parents asking repeatedly whether homework is finished.
- Feeling like school means giving up everything enjoyable.

### Success Looks Like

> "I know exactly what I need to do, I get it done before practice or afterwards, and I still have time for football and to talk to my girlfriend."

# Acceptance Criteria for UX Testing

## Student

Should be able to:

- Capture an assignment in under one minute.
- Understand today's plan within five seconds.
- Identify the next action immediately.
- Complete a planning session in under five minutes.

## Parent

Should be able to:

- Determine overall status in under thirty seconds.
- Identify whether intervention is needed.
- Avoid feeling compelled to inspect every assignment.

## Coach

Should be able to:

- Understand planning behaviours.
- Identify trends.
- Recommend adjustments to scaffolding without overwhelming the student.
